# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the PaperShift community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We welcome contributions from people of all backgrounds and levels of experience. Participants are expected to communicate professionally, act in good faith, and help maintain a constructive open-source community.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate of others.
- Communicating constructively and professionally.
- Welcoming different perspectives and experiences.
- Providing useful, actionable feedback.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respecting project maintainers, contributors, and users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Threats of violence or intimidation.
- Publishing private or personally identifying information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions, issues, pull requests, or project infrastructure.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate or unprofessional in a technical community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

If a participant engages in unacceptable behavior, maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including:

- Asking the participant to stop the behavior.
- Removing or editing inappropriate comments.
- Closing issues or pull requests.
- Temporarily restricting participation.
- Permanently restricting participation in the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainers through the repository's available contact or security-reporting channels.

Please include enough information to allow the maintainers to understand what happened and, where appropriate, links or other relevant context.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues and discussions.
- Pull requests and code reviews.
- Project documentation.
- Community communication associated with PaperShift.
- Other spaces where an individual is officially representing the PaperShift project.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by community standards commonly used across open-source projects, including the Contributor Covenant.

The project may adapt these guidelines as the PaperShift community evolves.
